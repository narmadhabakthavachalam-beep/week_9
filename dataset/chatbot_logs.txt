Week 9 – Chatbot Development
Domain: Education
Model: Llama 3.2 using Ollama

Observations:
1. The chatbot answers educational questions in simple English.
2. It can maintain conversation history during a session.
3. It saves user questions and chatbot responses with timestamps.
4. Response quality depends on the local language model.
5. Some answers may be incorrect or incomplete and should be verified.

Limitations:
1. The chatbot requires Ollama to be running.
2. The model may sometimes generate incorrect answers.
3. The chatbot currently runs in the command line.
